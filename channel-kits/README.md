# Channel kits

Everything two channels need, ready to copy into YouTube Studio:

1. **The Forgotten Lab** is the science/history story channel: the
   documentaries made with Wayne Factory, like Henrietta Lacks, the
   Radium Girls, Patient H.M. and Semmelweis.
2. **Hot Mic Moments** is the podcast clips channel: the Shorts made with
   Clip Factory's podcast mode.

Each folder has a **logo** (800x800), a **banner** (2560x1440, with all
text inside the area phones show) and a **watermark** (150x150). To
change the text or colours, edit `make_kits.py` and run
`python make_kits.py` again.

**Before you create a channel,** search the name and the @handle on
YouTube. If either is taken, use one of the alternatives listed.

**Where to upload the pictures:** YouTube Studio &rarr; **Customization**
&rarr; **Branding**:

| File | Goes in |
|---|---|
| `logo_800x800.png` | Picture |
| `banner_2560x1440.png` | Banner image |
| `watermark_150x150.png` | Video watermark, display time "Entire video" |

### Every video, the same way

- **Length:** 8-12 minutes. From 8 minutes, YouTube allows mid-video
  ads, and watch hours build up faster.
- **Story order:**
  1. Hook in the first 15 seconds.
  2. Who they were.
  3. The mystery or problem.
  4. The turning point.
  5. What happened to them.
  6. Why it matters today.
  7. A question for the comments.
- **Thumbnail:**
  - navy or dark background
  - **one** face or object
  - **3-4 words** in big cream or green letters, e.g. "THEY GLOWED IN THE
    DARK"
  - the same style every time, so people recognise the channel
- **Title:** curious but honest, e.g. "The Doctor They Locked Away for
  Saying 'Wash Your Hands'".
- **Description template:**
  ```
  [2-line hook about the story]

  [1 paragraph: what the video covers]

  Sources:
  - [link]
  - [link]

  Subscribe for a new forgotten story every Tuesday & Friday.
  ```
- **Pinned comment:** the question from the end of the video. Replies
  push the video to more people.
- **End screen:** the last 20 seconds. Add "Subscribe" plus "Best for
  viewer" video.

### Monetization path

- The ad tier needs **1,000 subscribers + 4,000 watch hours** in 12
  months.
- From **February 1, 2027**, new channels need **8,000** watch hours. Two
  long videos a week is the fastest way to get there before then.
- **Authenticity:** an AI voice over stock footage is what YouTube's
  "inauthentic content" rule targets. Researched stories with sources
  help a lot. Recording the voice yourself would make the channel much
  safer.
- **AI disclosure:** only tick **"Altered or synthetic content"** when a
  video shows **realistic** AI people or events. A narrated documentary
  doesn't need it.

### Every clip, the same way

Clip Factory's podcast mode already does most of this.
- **Only clip shows marked "Clipping allowed".** Check with **Can I clip
  them?**, then join their clipping program; many pay clippers per view.
  Follow each show's rules, e.g. "tag us".
- **Length:** 30-90 seconds. Use the split-screen layout, word-by-word
  captions, and a hook title on top.
- **Add your own value:** the hook title, the cut, and context in the
  title or description. YouTube's "reused content" rule refuses plain
  reuploads of other people's videos.
- **Title:** what happens in the clip, honest and with no swear words,
  e.g. "He Turned Down $1 Million for This Reason". Clip Factory keeps
  titles clean.
- **Description template:**
  ```
  [One line: what happens in this clip]

  From: [Show name] [@their handle]
  Full episode: [link]

  #podcast #shorts
  ```
- **Pinned comment:** a question, e.g. "Is he right?"

### Monetization path

- Shorts route: **1,000 subscribers + 10 million Shorts views in 90
  days**. From February 1, 2027, new channels will need 20 million.
- With clipping programs, the shows themselves often pay per view, so
  that can earn before YouTube monetization does.

  - the playlists as sections
