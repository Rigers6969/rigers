## HOW THEY PROFIT

**Backup names:** The Money Model · Behind The Brand · Profit Breakdown
**Channel description:** *"The real business behind the brands you use every day. How companies actually make their money - explained simply. New video every week."*
**Channel tags:** business, how companies make money, business explained, money, finance, companies, business model

### The formula (every video)
1. **0:00–0:20 Hook:** a surprising "it's not what you think" claim + a number on screen.
2. **0:20–1:30 The obvious answer** (what people think they pay for).
3. **1:30–6:00 The real money machine**, 3 parts, each with one big number on screen.
4. **6:00–8:00 The twist / the dark side** (who loses, what could break it).
5. **8:00–end:** one-line summary + question for the comments. No long outro.
**Visuals:** stock footage every 3–5 s, numbers and charts on screen, Money-gold grade, calm confident voice.
**Titles:** "How [Company] Really Makes Money", "Why [Company] Is Cheaper Than Everyone", "[Company] Isn't a [X] Company".

### Video 1 - How Costco REALLY Makes Money
**Hook (say this):** *"Costco sells you a hot dog for a dollar fifty and barely makes anything on it. On some products it barely breaks even. So how is it one of the most successful retailers on Earth? Because the thing Costco really sells... is the card in your wallet."*
**Parts:** (1) Cheap prices, low markups and huge volume → (2) Membership fees, a huge part of profit, most members renew → (3) Kirkland, the store brand → (4) The $1.50 hot dog as the "proof" of the promise.
**Check before recording:** Costco's latest annual report (membership fee income, renewal rate).

### Video 2 - McDonald's Isn't a Burger Company
**Hook:** *"McDonald's sells billions of burgers. But if you ask its own early leaders, burgers were never the real business. The real business... is the ground under your feet."*
**Parts:** (1) Ray Kroc's early franchising earned little → (2) Harry Sonneborn's real-estate idea → (3) Rent + fees from franchisees today → (4) Why franchisees can't easily leave.
**Check:** McDonald's annual report (franchised revenues, rent).

### Video 3 - How Spotify Pays Billions and Still Struggles
**Hook:** *"Spotify has hundreds of millions of users and makes billions every year. And for most of its life, it lost money. Here's where your monthly subscription actually goes."*
**Parts:** (1) Free vs Premium → (2) Most of the money goes to record labels and artists → (3) Podcasts, audiobooks, price rises → (4) The first profitable years.
**Check:** Spotify's latest yearly results (users, profit).

### Video 4 - How Google Makes Money From a Free Search
**Hook:** *"You've never paid Google a cent to search. Yet it's one of the most valuable companies in the world. That's because when something is free... someone else is paying for you to be there."*
**Parts:** (1) The ad auction behind every search → (2) Ads are most of the money (Search + YouTube) → (3) Data and Android → (4) What AI could change.
**Check:** Alphabet's annual report (share of revenue from ads).

### Video 5 - How Ryanair Sells €10 Flights and Still Makes Money
**Hook:** *"A €10 plane ticket should be impossible. Fuel, pilots and airports cost way more than that. So how does Ryanair make money? It's not the ticket. It's everything after it."*
**Parts:** (1) One plane model, fast turnarounds, small airports → (2) Extras: bags, seats, priority → (3) Airports sometimes pay *them* → (4) The angry-customer problem.
**Check:** Ryanair's annual report (ancillary revenue).

## WHAT IF LAB

**Backup names:** What Happens If · Body Facts Lab · Fact Machine
**Channel description:** *"What REALLY happens if you...? 30-second answers to the questions you always wondered about. New Short every day."*
**Channel tags:** what if, facts, science facts, human body, did you know, shorts

### The formula (every Short)
- **Frame 1:** the "What happens if…" question as a big title + a shocking picture.
- **0–3 s:** the question out loud. **3–25 s:** step by step, what happens, with a new picture every 2 s.
- **Last line:** a twist or myth-buster, then loop back to the question.
- **30–40 s**, one topic, no intro, no outro.

### Short 1 - What happens if you swallow gum?
*"What happens if you swallow chewing gum? You've heard it stays in your stomach for seven years. It doesn't. Your stomach can't break the gum base down - but it doesn't need to. Your gut simply moves it along with everything else. A day or two later... it's gone. The seven-year rule is a myth. Swallowing a LOT of gum at once, though? That can cause a blockage. So, what happens if you swallow gum?"*

### Short 2 - What happens if you stay awake for 24 hours?
*"What happens if you stay awake for 24 hours? After 17 hours, your reactions slow down. After 24 hours, studies say you can be as impaired as someone over the legal drinking limit for driving. Your brain starts taking 'micro-sleeps' - a few seconds of sleep you don't even notice. Your memory, mood and focus drop. One night of sleep fixes most of it. But do it every week... and it adds up. So, what happens if you stay awake for 24 hours?"*

### Short 3 - What happens if you fall into a black hole?
*"What happens if you fall into a black hole? Feet first, the pull on your feet gets much stronger than on your head. You get stretched - scientists literally call it spaghettification. Near a small black hole, that happens long before you reach it. Near a giant one, you could cross the edge without feeling anything... and never come back. And to someone watching from far away, you'd look like you slowed down and froze. So, what happens if you fall into a black hole?"*

### Short 4 - What happens if you crack your knuckles every day?
*"What happens if you crack your knuckles every day? That pop is a gas bubble forming in the fluid inside your joint. Your mom said it gives you arthritis. One doctor tested it on himself - he cracked the knuckles of only ONE hand for about 60 years. Result? No arthritis in either hand. He even won a prize for it. So cracking is probably harmless - unless it hurts. So, what happens if you crack your knuckles?"*

### Short 5 - What happens if you wake up a sleepwalker?
*"What happens if you wake up a sleepwalker? People say it can kill them, or give them a heart attack. That's a myth. What really happens: they wake up confused, scared, maybe a bit angry - because they have no idea how they got there. Doctors say the safest thing is to gently guide them back to bed. Waking them is OK - just do it softly. The real danger is the stairs, not you. So, what happens if you wake up a sleepwalker?"*
