**Day 1: Monday 2 November 2026. Day 400: Monday 6 December 2027.**

**Goals:**
- Learn to make **great animations yourself** in Premiere Pro and After Effects.
- Do **1 hour of school work every day**.
- Keep your channels growing while you learn.

## 1. The honest truth (read this once)

- **The videos the app makes are a start, not the finish.** The drawings I make in code are simple. Channels like Kurzgesagt, MagnatesMedia or Johnny Harris look great because a person animated them by hand. **That person can be you.** It takes months, not days. After 400 days of practice, you can be really good.
- **Your time is small,** with school 8:00–13:30 and work until 22:00. So every minute in this plan has one job. If your work hours change, tell me and I will change the plan.
- **School is not optional.** If your grades drop, the plan has failed, even if a video goes viral. The school hour comes **first** every day.
- **Keep posting while you learn.** Wayne Factory keeps making Shorts. Your animations replace them step by step.
- **The software costs money.** Adobe Premiere Pro + After Effects need a Creative Cloud plan. Ask Adobe for the **student price**, which is much cheaper. Free option: **DaVinci Resolve** (free, with Fusion for animation) and **Blender** (free, 2D and 3D). The plan works with both; the tutorials below are for Adobe.
- **Your PC:** your i7-8700 and GTX 1630 are fine for 2D animation. Check your RAM (Settings → System → About). After Effects wants **16 GB**, and 32 GB is better. If you have 8 GB, RAM is the first thing to buy (around 40–60 euro).

## 3. What you learn: the animation path (400 days)

**The rule: watch 1 thing, make 1 thing.** Never only watch. Every tutorial gets copied in your own project the same week.

### Phase 1 — Days 1–30 (2 Nov – 1 Dec 2026): Premiere Pro basics
- **Learn:** cutting, the timeline, audio levels, **keyframes** (position, scale, opacity), captions, export settings for Shorts.
- **Watch:** Gavin Herman's beginner Premiere course (one chapter a night), then Kyler Holland's videos on the newest Premiere features.
- **Project of the month:** remake our **Eiffel Tower Short** in Premiere yourself. Use real photos with slow zooms (Ken Burns), and captions that pop.
- **Done when:** you can make a 30-second Short from photos + voice in under 2 hours.

### Phase 2 — Days 31–100 (2 Dec 2026 – 9 Feb 2027): After Effects foundations
- **Learn, one per week:**
  1. Interface, layers, keyframes
  2. **Easing and the Graph Editor** — the secret of smooth animation
  3. Shape layers + Trim Paths (lines that draw themselves)
  4. Text animators (words that slam, type and bounce)
  5. Masks and track mattes
  6. Parenting + null objects
  7. Pre-comps
  8. Motion blur
  9. Sound design (whoosh, hit, riser)
  10. A full 15-second piece
- **Watch:** Adobe's free After Effects workshop (Kyle Hamrick), Ben Marriott (beginner videos), Jake in Motion (tips and tricks).
- **Weekly mini projects:** the Paper Trail logo animation · a money counter · a map line from London to Paris · a "SOLD" stamp slam · a newspaper headline zoom · a bar chart that grows.
- **Done when:** your stamp slam and counter look **smoother** than the ones in our Shorts.

### Phase 3 — Days 101–200 (10 Feb – 20 May 2027): 2D motion graphics for your channels
- **Learn:**
  - flat-design scenes made from shapes (SonduckFilm's Kurzgesagt-style tutorials)
  - 2.5D camera and parallax (photos with depth)
  - Flat Pack FX tutorials (newspapers, graphs, timelines)
  - **basic expressions:** `wiggle`, `loopOut`, a number counter
  - colour palettes and fonts
- **Project:** **one fully animated 30-second Short every week**, made by you. Post it on Paper Trail or The Forgotten Lab.
- **Done when:** your own animated Shorts get the same or a better **average % viewed** than the app's Shorts.

### Phase 4 — Days 201–300 (21 May – 28 Aug 2027): characters, maps and speed
- **Learn:**
  - **Duik Angela** (free character rigging): a simple character that walks, points and talks
  - 3D layers + camera for maps and "flying" over a scene
  - **Essential Graphics / MOGRT templates**: your own template pack (titles, captions, counter, map, stamp) so a Short takes 2–3 hours, not 8
- **Summer = more time:** do 2 animated Shorts a week, plus your first **animated long video** (5–8 minutes) for Paper Trail.
- **Project:** a channel **mascot / host character** for Paper Trail or The Forgotten Lab.

### Phase 5 — Days 301–400 (29 Aug – 6 Dec 2027): your own style
- **Each month:** pick 30 seconds from a great animated channel and **copy it exactly** in your project. This is practice only; **never upload a copy**. Then use what you learned in your own video.
- **Make:** 2–3 animated Shorts a week + 1 animated long video a month.
- **Build a portfolio:** your 10 best animations in one reel. With this you can also **earn money as a freelance animator** (Fiverr, Upwork, local businesses). This can pay faster than YouTube.
- **Day 400 goal:** you make a 30-second animated Short in about 2 hours, it looks professional, and your channels grow from it.

## 4. The school hour (every day, before anything else)

1. **Homework first** (whatever is due tomorrow).
2. **No homework?** Then:
   - 10 min: read today's notes once
   - 40 min: exercises in your **weakest subject**
   - 10 min: English (it helps school *and* your YouTube scripts)
3. **Before a test:** the whole hour goes to that subject, plus the exam-week rules in section 2.
4. **Phone in another room.** One hour of real focus beats three hours with the phone.
5. **Every month:** look at your grades. If one subject drops, the school hour goes to that subject until it's back up.

## 10. Rules for 400 days

1. **School hour first, sleep second, animation third.**
2. **Watch 1, make 1.** Every tutorial gets used the same week.
3. **Post on schedule, even if it isn't perfect.** Animation 50 will be much better than animation 1.
4. **Never upload someone else's work or a copy.** Copy only to practise, privately.
5. **Look at the numbers on Sunday, not every hour.**
6. **Missed a day? Don't miss two.**
7. **Exam weeks are school weeks.** Keep 5 Shorts ready before each one.

You don't become a great animator in one month. You get there by being a little better every week for 400 days, and this plan is built for that.
