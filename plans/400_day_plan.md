# The 400-Day Plan

**Day 1: Monday 2 November 2026. Day 400: Monday 6 December 2027.**

Goal: become a strong editor in Premiere Pro and After Effects, get your channels monetized, and push the best ones toward 100,000 subscribers, while you go to school and work.

---

## 1. The honest truth first (read this once)

- **You have about 16–17 free hours a week.** School 8:00–13:30 and work until 22:00 leave about 1.5–2 hours on weekdays, plus the weekend.
- **Six channels to 100K each in 400 days is not possible** with that time. Even full-time YouTubers with teams rarely do it. If you start all six at once, they will all stay small.
- **What is possible:** get **2–3 channels monetized**, and give **one or two** a real chance at 100K. That is the plan below.
- **The secret is focus, then automation.** Your apps (Wayne Factory, Clip Factory, the new Researcher) do the boring work. Your time goes into what makes videos win: **the idea, the title, the thumbnail and the first 30 seconds.**
- **Money:** long business and finance videos (Paper Trail) usually earn much more per view than Shorts. Shorts are great for subscribers but pay little. So **Paper Trail is your money channel.**
- **Clip channels have a risk.** Hot Mic Moments and Chat Lost It use other people's videos. YouTube often refuses monetization for "reused content" unless you add something new: your own voice-over intro, commentary or a story. Clip Factory's narrator helps with this. Never upload clips without adding your own part.

### What "monetized" means (YouTube Partner Program)
You need **1,000 subscribers** plus one of these:
- **4,000 watch hours** on long videos in the last 12 months, or
- **10 million Shorts views** in the last 90 days.

For long-video channels, the watch hours are the hard part. For Shorts channels, the 10 million views are.

### Kill or change rules (very important)
- After **20 long videos** on a channel: if most videos get **under 3% click-through rate** or **under 30% average view duration**, the problem is the **titles/thumbnails** (low CTR) or the **hook and story** (low duration). Fix that before posting more.
- After **60 days** with almost no growth, change the topic style. Do not keep doing the same thing.
- If one channel suddenly grows fast, **move time to it.** Winners get more time; losers get paused.

## 4. The 400 days, phase by phase

### Phase 1: Days 1–30 (2 Nov – 1 Dec 2026) — Set up and learn the basics
**Goal: the tools work, you know Premiere basics, and the first videos are out.**
- Week 1:
  - Check your PC: Windows 11 24H2? How much RAM? (After Effects needs 16 GB.) Install Premiere Pro and After Effects.
  - Run the Researcher for the first time. Change the search words on "Channels & words" to your taste.
  - Make the channel art and banner for Paper Trail and Hot Mic Moments.
- Learn: Gavin Herman's "Learn Premiere Pro in 10 minutes", then his full course, **one chapter a night**.
- Make: **4 Paper Trail videos** (use Wayne Factory for the script, voice and stock footage, then fix the edit in Premiere). **Hot Mic Moments: start posting daily** with Clip Factory.
- Read: 3–4 books.

### Phase 2: Days 31–100 (2 Dec 2026 – 9 Feb 2027) — Build the rhythm
**Goal: posting on time every week without stress.**
- Paper Trail: 1 long video/week, and **2/week from day 80** if it is easy. Cut 3 Shorts from each long video.
- **History starts on day 61.**
- Learn in Premiere (from your report): Ken Burns photo moves, the newspaper highlight, the money counter, map lines, Auto Ducking, the 2026 single-word captions, Auto Reframe.
- Make your own **templates** (title style, lower third, money counter) so every new video is faster.
- Read: 1 book/week.
- **Target by day 100:** Paper Trail 15+ long videos, Hot Mic Moments 100+ Shorts, History 5+ videos.

### Phase 3: Days 101–200 (10 Feb – 20 May 2027) — Better quality, first money
**Goal: first channel monetized, and you start After Effects.**
- **Chat Lost It started on day 91**; keep it automated.
- Learn After Effects: Adobe's free Kyle Hamrick workshop, then Ben Marriott. Then the exact graphics each channel needs: Flat Pack FX (newspapers, graphs, timelines), money counter in After Effects, a map without plugins.
- Every long video gets **3 "signature" graphics** made in After Effects. This is what makes your channel look better than the others.
- **The Forgotten Lab may start on day 151** if everything else is on time.
- **Target by day 200:** Paper Trail close to or at monetization (1,000 subs + 4,000 hours). Hot Mic Moments 1,000+ subscribers.

### Phase 4: Days 201–300 (21 May – 28 Aug 2027) — Double down on what works
**Goal: 2 channels monetized; the best channel grows fast.**
- Summer: fewer school hours, so use the extra time on your **best** channel, not on new channels.
- **The Albanian channel may start on day 181**: translate your 10 best documentaries.
- Make **series** (for example "Biggest Frauds of the 2020s", 5 parts). Series make people binge.
- With the first YouTube money: **pay for help, not courses.** For example a thumbnail designer, or a part-time editor for the clip channels.
- **Target by day 300:** 2 channels monetized, best channel 20K–40K subscribers if it hit.

### Phase 5: Days 301–400 (29 Aug – 6 Dec 2027) — Push to 100K
**Goal: one channel as close to 100K as possible; everything else on autopilot.**
- Look at your 10 best videos. Make **more of exactly that**: same topic type, same title style, same length.
- Remake your best old videos better (new title, new thumbnail, new hook).
- Collaborate: guest voice-overs, shout-outs with channels your size.
- **Target by day 400:** 2–3 channels monetized. One channel at 100K **if** a few videos go big. 100K is a stretch goal, not a promise.

## 5. The weekly plan

| Day | Main block (22:15–23:45) |
|---|---|
| Monday | **Script + voice** for this week's long video (Researcher idea → Wayne Factory script → fix the hook yourself). |
| Tuesday | **Edit part 1**: B-roll, pacing, cut every silence. |
| Wednesday | **Learning night**: one tutorial from your report, and use it in this week's video. |
| Thursday | **Edit part 2**: graphics (counter, newspaper, map), sound effects, music. |
| Friday | Light: check Clip Factory Shorts (10 min each channel), then rest. |
| Saturday (5 h) | Finish the long video. Make 3 Shorts from it. Second long video when you are in 2/week mode. |
| Sunday (3–4 h) | Thumbnails (make 2 per video and test), titles, schedule uploads, **weekly review**, plan next week. |

**The 80/20 of editing:** most viewers leave in the first 30 seconds. Spend the most time on **the first 30 seconds** and **the thumbnail + title**. Then the rest.

## 7. Weekly and monthly review

**Every Sunday (30 min), in YouTube Studio:**
1. Which video had the highest **click-through rate**? Why (title, thumbnail)?
2. Where do people leave? (the retention graph) Fix that in the next video.
3. Write 3 lessons in one note. Read last week's lessons before you start.
4. Pick next week's ideas from the Researcher's saved ideas.

**Every month (1 hour):** compare channels. Give more time to the one growing fastest. Pause the one that grows the least, if it has had a fair chance (60+ days).

## 10. Rules for 400 days

1. **School first, sleep second, YouTube third.** A plan you quit in month 2 is worth nothing.
2. **Post on schedule, even when it is not perfect.** Video 50 will be much better than video 1.
3. **One new skill per week, used the same week.**
4. **Never upload clips without your own added part** (voice intro, commentary).
5. **Don't buy courses.** Free tutorials are enough. Spend money only on help that saves time.
6. **Look at numbers every Sunday, not every hour.**
7. **Missed a day? Don't miss two.**

You don't get rich from one viral video. You get there by being a little better every week for 400 days. This plan is built for that.
