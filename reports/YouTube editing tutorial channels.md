# Learn to Edit All Six Channels

**Short answer:** Start with two free programs. Use **CapCut** for all your Shorts (Hot Mic Moments, Chat Lost It, and the Shorts from your other channels). Use **DaVinci Resolve (free)** for your long documentaries (Paper Trail, History, The Forgotten Lab). Your graphics card (GTX 1630, 4 GB) is **exactly at the minimum** for Resolve, Premiere Pro and After Effects, so paying for Adobe is a bad first step. Learn Resolve from **Casey Faris** and **Kevin Stratvert**, and learn CapCut from **Primal Video**. For style, the best teachers are **Boone Loves Video** and **Flat Pack FX** (maps, for History), **SonduckFilm** (documentary graphics, for Paper Trail) and **Ben Marriott** (clean animation, for The Forgotten Lab). All four teach in **After Effects**, so they are a "level 2" step. For story and retention, start with **George Blackman** and **Film Booth** (long videos) and **Jenny Hoyos** and **Kallaway** (Shorts). We found **no good Albanian-language editing channel**, so learn from English videos with Albanian auto-translated subtitles. Be careful with the numbers: every subscriber count below is a snapshot from a tracker site, and YouTube pages could not be opened during this research. Check each channel yourself before you trust a number.

## How to read this report

- **Subscriber numbers** come from tracker sites (Social Blade, vidIQ, HypeAuditor and others) on the date shown. They are not live numbers. "Not verified" means we found no reliable number.
- **YouTube, Social Blade and Reddit could not be opened** during the research. Every link came from search results. No link was invented. A few channel links were built from the channel's @handle and are marked "(built from handle)".

## Which software: free Resolve plus free CapCut fits your PC best

All four programs will install on your PC. But your 4 GB graphics card sits **at the floor, not above it**. Resolve 21 asks for at least 4 GB of graphics memory ([Blackmagic](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/support/content/readme/dfbb6e4233c144d1b328c109c7ebc11e)). Premiere Pro 26 also asks for 4 GB ([Adobe](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/get-started/technical-requirements/adobe-premiere-pro-technical-requirements.html)). After Effects asks for 4 GB and notes 8 GB ([Adobe](https://helpx.adobe.com/fi/after-effects/desktop/get-started/technical-requirements/system-requirements.html)).

### Your PC compared with each program

| Program | Windows needed | RAM needed | Fit for your PC |
|---|---|---|---|

Sources: [Resolve 21 readme](https://www.blackmagicdesign.com/support/content/readme/dfbb6e4233c144d1b328c109c7ebc11e), [Premiere requirements](https://helpx.adobe.com/premiere/desktop/get-started/technical-requirements/adobe-premiere-pro-technical-requirements.html), [After Effects requirements](https://helpx.adobe.com/fi/after-effects/desktop/get-started/technical-requirements/system-requirements.html), [CapCut Help](https://www.capcut.com/help/no-2k-or-4k-option-for-export).

**Two things we do not know about your PC:** how much RAM it has, and which Windows version it runs. Check both first. They decide a lot. Adobe's 2026 apps **need Windows 11 24H2** ([Adobe](https://helpx.adobe.com/sg/premiere-pro/system-requirements.html)). Resolve and After Effects **need 16 GB RAM** as a minimum.

Good news and bad news about your hardware:
- Your i7-8700 is an 8th-gen Intel chip. It meets Adobe's "Intel 6th Gen or newer" rule.
- Your GTX 1630 can export H.264 and HEVC videos with its hardware encoder ([NVIDIA](https://developer.nvidia.com/video-encode-decode-support-matrix?ncid=em-prod-816193)).
- Free Resolve on Windows reportedly **does not use the graphics card to play H.264/HEVC footage** ([Puget Systems](https://pugetsystems.com/pugetbench/creators/davinci-resolve)). A Blackmagic forum post disagrees. Either way, make **proxy files** so playback stays smooth. Kevin Stratvert's Resolve video (below) has a chapter on proxies.

**When Premiere + After Effects makes sense:** only if you move to Windows 11 24H2, ideally add RAM (16–32 GB) and a graphics card with 8 GB, and want the biggest pool of map and chart tutorials. Almost every strong documentary-graphics tutorial uses After Effects. Premiere 26.3 also has built-in word-by-word captions now ([Kyler Holland](https://www.kylerholland.com/blog/single-word-captions-premiere-pro-tutorial)). ## Start here: a simple learning order

This order is our suggestion. It starts with quick, cheap wins and leaves the heavy, paid tools for later.

1. **Check your PC.** Find your RAM and Windows version. Install **CapCut** and **DaVinci Resolve (free)**.
2. **Learn CapCut.** Use Primal Video's 2026 CapCut desktop guide, or Kevin Stratvert's CapCut video. Then watch one word-by-word caption video. Post your first Hot Mic Moments and Chat Lost It Shorts.
3. **Learn hooks.** Watch Kallaway's "6 hooks" video and one Jenny Hoyos interview. Use them on every Short.
4. **Learn Resolve.** Watch Kevin Stratvert's 38-minute Resolve video first (set up proxies). Then do Casey Faris's 5-hour course, one chapter a day.
5. **Learn scripts and retention.** Watch George Blackman and Film Booth. Follow vidIQ's rule: 3 test videos, change one thing at a time.
6. **Learn simple documentary graphics in Resolve.** Kevin Stratvert's Fusion video, the MagnatesMedia DaVinci guide, and the Vox-in-Resolve video. Watch Johnny Harris's "How I Make My Maps" once to understand the style.
7. **Later, only if a channel is growing:** learn After Effects. Start with Ben Marriott (10 minutes, then 60 minutes). Then Boone Loves Video or Flat Pack FX for maps (needs the paid GEOlayers plugin), and SonduckFilm for documentary graphics. Before this step, move to Windows 11 24H2 and check that you have at least 16 GB RAM.
8. **Any time, for the Albanian channel:** turn on Albanian auto-translate subtitles, watch the Albanian CapCut video, and always check Albanian auto captions by hand.

## Conclusion

The biggest gap is between the free tool and where the tutorials live. Resolve is free and fits your PC, but almost every strong map, chart and newspaper tutorial is made in After Effects, often with a paid plugin. The smart move is to earn your way into After Effects: grow on free tools first, and pay for Adobe and GEOlayers only when the History or Paper Trail channel proves it can grow. By then you may also need a better PC, because After Effects is the program most likely to struggle on a 4 GB graphics card.

For the clip channels, software is not the hard part. CapCut and a caption style can be learned in a week. Success depends on the first two seconds of each clip and on whether you are allowed to post the clips at all, and that second question still needs an answer. For the Albanian channel, the lack of Albanian editing teachers is a problem for learning, but it could also be your best content idea.
