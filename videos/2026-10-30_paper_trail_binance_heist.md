# Paper Trail: the $6.3 billion Binance heist

**Why this story:** It happened on 29–30 Oct 2026 and is reported as the biggest crypto theft in history. Everyone is searching for it right now.

**Make it TODAY:** news videos get most of their views in the first 48 hours.

**Before you publish:** the numbers are still changing, so check one fresh headline (Reuters, AP or Al Jazeera) first. If a number changed, fix it in the title or script.

## Facts (each one says who reported it, so the video stays safe)
- **Binance says:** about **$6.3 billion** was affected, from its hot wallets.
- **Timing:** the attack started around 20:05 UTC on 29 Oct, with a second wave around 23:05 UTC. Most of the money left in about **20 minutes**.
- **Frozen:** nearly **$3 billion** was frozen on-chain, mostly by Tether (about 1.57B USDT) and Circle (about 800M USDC).
- **Still missing:** about **$3.3 billion** is unaccounted for.
- **Cause, according to Binance's early findings:** a DevOps engineer's laptop was hacked, which let the attacker send withdrawals.
- **Attribution:** TRM Labs (a blockchain analytics firm) linked the attack to North Korea's "TraderTraitor" group. *No government has confirmed this yet, so always say "linked by TRM Labs".*
- **Binance's response:** it says it will cover user losses (it set aside over $6.34B) and it paused deposits and withdrawals.
- **Market:** Bitcoin fell to about $76,000 on the news.
- **Comparison:** the old record was Bybit, about $1.5B in Feb 2025, which the FBI blamed on North Korea. This one is about **4 times bigger**.
