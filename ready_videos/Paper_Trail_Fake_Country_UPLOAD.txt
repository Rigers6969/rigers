DESCRIPTION
In 1822, Gregor MacGregor convinced London investors and settlers that he ruled 'Poyais', a rich nation in Central America. He sold land, raised a £200,000 loan and printed his own money. The country did not exist. Around 250 settlers sailed there - most of them never came home.
