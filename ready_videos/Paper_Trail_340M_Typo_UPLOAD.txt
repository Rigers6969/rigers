TITLE
The $340 Million Typo ⌨️ #shorts

DESCRIPTION
In December 2005, a trader at Mizuho Securities meant to sell 1 share of J-Com for 610,000 yen - and typed 610,000 shares for 1 yen. That was about 40 times more shares than the company had. The cancel order failed, traders bought everything, and Mizuho lost about $340 million. A court later ordered the Tokyo Stock Exchange to pay back roughly a quarter.
