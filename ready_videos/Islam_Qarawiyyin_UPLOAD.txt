The World’s Oldest School Was Founded by a Woman 🕌 #shorts

DESCRIPTION
In 859, Fatima al-Fihri used her inheritance to found al-Qarawiyyin in Fez, Morocco - a mosque and a place of learning. Guinness World Records lists it as the oldest existing, continually operating educational institution in the world.
