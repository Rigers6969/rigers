This Animal Survived Space 🐻 #shorts

DESCRIPTION
Tardigrades (water bears) are about half a millimetre long. Dried out into a 'tun', they can survive extreme cold, heat and radiation. In 2007, on the FOTON-M3 mission, tardigrades spent 10 days exposed to open space - and many survived.
