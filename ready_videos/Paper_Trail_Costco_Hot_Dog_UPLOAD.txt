TITLE
Why This Hot Dog Still Costs $1.50 🌭 #shorts

DESCRIPTION
Costco's hot dog and soda combo has cost $1.50 since 1985. When a CEO suggested raising it, co-founder Jim Sinegal reportedly said: "If you raise the hot dog, I will kill you." Costco built its own hot dog plants and switched soda suppliers to keep the price - because the hot dog isn't the product. Membership fees are a huge part of Costco's profit, and around 90% of members renew.
