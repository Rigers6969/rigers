Would You Obey? 65% Did ⚡ #shorts

DESCRIPTION
In 1961 at Yale, psychologist Stanley Milgram asked ordinary people to give electric shocks to a 'learner' whenever he got an answer wrong. In his first study, 65% went all the way to 450 volts when a man in a lab coat told them to continue. The shocks were fake and the learner was an actor.
