How it works

From link to posted

  1. Send a link

    A YouTube, Twitch, Kick or Drive link to your video, and a clip whose style you like.

  2. We pick the moments

    We find the parts with a strong hook and cut them to 25–60 seconds, never mid-sentence.

  3. You get the clips

    First clips within 48 hours, each with its title, cover and hashtags.

  4. Ask for changes

    Two rounds of changes are included. Then you post, or we schedule them for you.

Questions

Before you order

How fast do I get my clips?

The first clips arrive within 48 hours of your link. On the Agency plan, within 24 hours.

Which platforms are the clips for?

YouTube Shorts, TikTok, Instagram Reels and Facebook Reels: vertical 1080×1920, or 4K if you ask.

Can I choose the caption style?

Yes. Pick one of our styles, or send us a clip you like and we match its captions, layout and length.

What if I don't like a clip?

Tell us what to change. Two rounds of changes are included in every plan.

Do I need to own the video?

Yes. Send only videos you made or have permission to use. We don't edit movies, TV shows or other people's content without permission.

How do I pay?

After your free sample, we send you a payment request. Monthly plans are paid at the start of each month.
