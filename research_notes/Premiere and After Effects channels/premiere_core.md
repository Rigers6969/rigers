# Best English-language YouTube channels for learning Adobe Premiere Pro (beginner to intermediate), as of 26 Oct 2026

**How this was researched (read first):** In this session, direct page fetches were blocked. WebFetch and curl could not reach youtube.com, socialblade.com, vidiq.com, motionarray.com, classcentral.com or reddit.com. All facts below come from WebSearch result pages and their snippets (searched 26 Oct 2026). The search tool shows the page and snippet but often not the uploader, so **the channel behind many video URLs could not be confirmed**. Each such URL is marked "channel not verified." Subscriber counts are third-party tracker snapshots and come with their date where the snippet gave one. No Reddit r/premiere threads came up in search.

**Version context:** Adobe renamed "Premiere Pro" to "Adobe Premiere" with version 26.0 on 20 Jan 2026 — [Wikipedia: Adobe Premiere Pro](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Adobe_Premiere_Pro). Search for both "Premiere Pro 2026" and "Adobe Premiere" when looking for new tutorials. Releases this year: 26.0 (January 2026), 26.3 (June 2026, single-word captions) and 26.5 (September 2026, Paper Edit and a Generative Media tool) — [Adobe Community: What's New in Premiere 26.5](https://community.adobe.com/announcements-727/what-s-new-in-adobe-premiere-26-5-september-2026-1641187); [Kyler Holland: 26.3 What's New](https://www.kylerholland.com/blog/premiere-pro-june-2026-whats-new); [Kyler Holland: 26.0 What's New](https://www.kylerholland.com/blog/premiere-pro-january-2026-whats-new).

## Q1. Channel profiles: name, URL, subscribers (source and date), 2025–2026 activity, teaching style, paid courses

### Takeaway
Seven of the 14 suggested channels are strong, verifiable Premiere teaching channels: Justin Odisho, Premiere Gal, Premiere Basics, Cinecom.net, Gavin Herman, Adobe Video and Motion Array Tutorials. Kyler Holland is a new find that is very relevant, because he covers each 2026 Premiere release. Vince Opra and Olufemii are useful but less Premiere-focused now. Joseph | Video Editing, Matthias Dangl and Kevin Stratvert could not be verified as good Premiere teaching channels. Video Editing With Tyler White is small and its content found is older.
