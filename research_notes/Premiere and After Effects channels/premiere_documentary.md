# Premiere Pro tutorials for faceless documentary editing (Paper Trail, History channel, The Forgotten Lab)

Research date: 2026-10-26. Scope: English-language YouTube tutorials and channels for documentary-style editing inside Adobe Premiere Pro, using After Effects through Dynamic Link only where needed.

**Important limits on this research (read first):**
- From this research environment, youtube.com, socialblade.com, reddit.com and most blogs could not be opened directly. WebFetch returned DNS errors for youtube.com, socialblade.com and blog.motionarray.com. The proxy refused curl to youtube.com and noembed.com with a 403, and Reddit is blocked in search. All video titles and URLs below come from web-search result listings, which give each video's real URL and title. **Search results almost never name the uploader channel.** Unless a finding says otherwise, every video is marked "uploader not confirmed". Nobody watched the videos, so how clear each one is for a beginner was **not verified**.
- Video ages marked "approx." come from the search tool's "N days old" metadata, converted to dates from 2026-10-26. Treat them as rough.

## Q1. Which channels and videos teach these techniques in Premiere Pro (title, URL, uploader)? Which are clear for a basic-English beginner?

### Takeaway
No single channel covers the whole "faceless documentary in Premiere" toolkit. The material is spread across many small, single-technique videos: Ken Burns, parallax, newspaper highlight, text match cut, map line, Google Earth zoom, graph line and film grain. Motion Array's written guide plus a set of "like Vox / Johnny Harris in Premiere Pro" videos are the most on-target. Most MagnatesMedia-style tutorials found use After Effects, DaVinci Resolve or Filmora, not Premiere.

  4. Fit the music bed with Remix.
  5. Add whoosh, riser and hit layers on transitions and reveals.
