## Q1. Which 8–10 English-language After Effects channels are best for documentary and explainer graphics? (name, URL, subscribers with source and date, active 2025–26, level, sells courses or plugins)

### Takeaway
For faceless documentary and explainer work, the most useful channels are Flat Pack FX (Vox-style timelines, newspapers, graphs, maps), Boone Loves Video (GEOlayers maps), Ben Marriott (flat 2D shape animation of the Kurzgesagt kind) and Jake In Motion (fundamentals). For a beginner, Adobe Video's free Kyle Hamrick workshop is the best way in. SonduckFilm, Dope Motions, School of Motion, ECAbrams and Motion Array Tutorials are useful extras. Video Copilot and Cinecom.net are big channels but suit this niche less well: Video Copilot is VFX-focused and I found no recent uploads, and Cinecom covers general filmmaking.

## Q4. Will After Effects 2026 run acceptably on an i7-8700 PC with a GTX 1630 4 GB?

### Takeaway
On paper the CPU and GPU meet the published minimums. 2026 guides list an NVIDIA GPU of Maxwell generation or newer with at least 4 GB VRAM, and the 25.x requirements list an Intel 6th-gen or newer CPU with AVX2. The GTX 1630 is a very weak card, though: 4 GB on a 64-bit bus, and slower than a GTX 1050 Ti in games. Expect workable 1080p 2D motion graphics (shapes, text, charts, GEOlayers maps) with reduced preview resolution and good caching, but slow work with heavy GPU effects, 3D and 4K. System RAM, which matters most for previews, was not specified (16 GB minimum).

### Cited Findings
- Adobe's current requirements page covers the 26.x line: 26.0 (Jan 2026), 26.2 (Apr 2026), 26.2.1 (Apr 2026), 26.3 (Jun 2026) and 26.5 (Sep 2026). The Windows table values were not retrieved. — [Adobe system requirements](https://helpx.adobe.com/after-effects/desktop/get-started/technical-requirements/system-requirements.html)
- Third-party 2026 summaries: Windows 10 22H2 or later, or Windows 11; 16 GB RAM minimum (32 GB+ for 4K); NVIDIA GPU of Maxwell generation or newer with at least 4 GB VRAM. **Conflict:** another guide lists 8 GB RAM, and older pages list 2 GB VRAM. These look out of date. — [CGDirector](https://www.cgdirector.com/adobe-after-effects-system-requirements/); [FixThePhoto](https://fixthephoto.com/adobe-after-effects-system-requirements.html)
- After Effects 25.x CPU minimum: Intel 6th Gen or newer, or AMD Ryzen 1000 or newer, with AVX2 support. — [Adobe 25.x requirements (localized)](https://helpx.adobe.com/se/after-effects/desktop/get-started/technical-requirements/2025.html)
- After Effects 24.x GPU guidance: 4 GB of GPU memory for HD and some 4K media, 6 GB or more for 4K and higher. — [Adobe 24.x requirements (localized)](https://helpx.adobe.com/dk/after-effects/desktop/get-started/technical-requirements/2024.html)
- GTX 1630: 4 GB GDDR6 on a 64-bit bus, about half the bandwidth of the GTX 1650. In Hardware Unboxed's tests it averaged 37 fps, against 43 fps for the GTX 1050 Ti and 60 fps for the RX 6400. — [Noticias3D](https://www.noticias3d.com/noticia/89219/hardware-unboxed-pone-prueba-geforce-gtx-1630-considera-mala.html); [Gizmodo](https://gizmodo.com/nvidia-geforce-gtx-1630-release-date-reviews-nvidia-amd-1849117905)
- I found no After Effects benchmark for the GTX 1630. An Adobe Community thread on a similar GTX 1650 Max-Q laptop reports slow renders, and replies point to the footage resolution (4K) as a factor. — [Adobe Community](https://community.adobe.com/t5/after-effects-discussions/settings-for-a-nvidia-gtx-1650-with-max-q-design/td-p/13823833)
- Adobe performance tips:
  - Put the disk cache on a separate fast SSD, on a different drive from the footage.
  - Lower the composition or preview resolution, or use Auto resolution.
  - Skip frames in the Preview panel.
  - Pre-render nested comps.
  - Animate first and add heavy effects last.
  - Enable hardware-accelerated panels.
  - Running Premiere at the same time reduces the RAM After Effects has for previews, which matters for Dynamic Link.
  — [Adobe: Improve performance](https://helpx.adobe.com/uk/after-effects/using/improve-performance.html); [Adobe: Memory and storage](https://helpx.adobe.com/uk/after-effects/using/memory-storage1.html)
- Community tips:
  - The Preview panel's resolution is independent of the Viewer's resolution.
  - Shift+Numpad 0 previews every other frame.
  - Transcode H.264/MP4 to an intermediate codec such as ProRes.
  - Some users fixed freezes by turning off "Hardware Accelerate Composition, Layer, and Footage Panels". Test this on your own machine.
  — [Adobe Community: best settings](https://community.adobe.com/questions-529/what-are-the-best-settings-to-optimize-performance-and-ram-preview-60637); [Adobe Community: AE 2025 freezes](https://community.adobe.com/questions-529/after-effects-2025-freezes-when-using-video-assets-59409)
- There is an Adobe Community thread about speeding up previews in After Effects 26 specifically. Its content was not retrieved. — [Adobe Community](https://community.adobe.com/questions-529/can-you-tell-me-how-to-speed-up-the-preview-in-after-effects-26-1559954)

### Inferences
- The i7-8700 (Intel 8th gen, 6 cores/12 threads, AVX2) is above the 6th-gen minimum. The GTX 1630 (a GTX 16-series card, which is newer than Maxwell) with 4 GB meets the minimum GPU line but sits at the very bottom of it. It fits Adobe's "4 GB for HD" guidance, so stay at 1080p timelines.
- The documentary graphics in this brief (shape-layer charts, counters, text, 2D maps, parallax photos) depend mostly on the CPU, RAM and disk cache, not the GPU. They should be workable at Half or Quarter preview resolution. GPU-heavy work such as the Advanced 3D renderer, heavy blur or glow stacks, 4K footage and big GEOlayers satellite tiles will be slow.
- 32 GB RAM and an NVMe SSD disk cache are likely to help more than anything else. Keeping Premiere closed while doing heavy work in After Effects also helps, because Premiere takes RAM from After Effects previews.
- Keep the NVIDIA Studio driver current. This is general practice and was not confirmed for the GTX 1630 this session.

### Gaps
- Adobe's exact 26.x Windows minimum and recommended table (CPU, RAM, VRAM, driver) was not retrieved. The figures above come from third-party 2026 summaries and Adobe's 24.x/25.x pages.
- No After Effects benchmark or user report specific to the GTX 1630 was found. Reddit could not be checked.
- The user's RAM and storage (SSD or HDD) are unknown. They are the main factors in preview performance.
