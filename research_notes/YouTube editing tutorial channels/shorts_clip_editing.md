**Caption technique facts (useful for the report writer)**
- Hormozi look in CapCut: Montserrat Extra Bold, black uppercase text, a yellow background behind the caption with roundness around 20%. The tutorial also says CapCut auto captions are "very accurate and very fast" — [GoTranscript transcript of a CapCut tutorial](https://gotranscript.com/public/mastering-youtube-shorts-capcut-tutorial-for-ali-abdaal-alex-hormozi-and-cody-sanchez-styles)
- Hormozi look in Premiere Pro: transcribe in the Text panel, then create captions with a maximum of ~15 characters, a minimum duration of ~2 seconds and a double line. The source is a vendor (Submagic) — [Submagic blog](https://www.submagic.co/blog/how-to-make-alex-hormozi-captions)
- CapCut has two word-by-word modes. "Highlight current word" keeps the phrase on screen and highlights the spoken word. "One word only" replaces the previous word each time — [capcutguide.com](https://capcutguide.com/capcut-word-by-word-captions/)
- On a phone-sized vertical clip, one or two short lines per caption card read far better than a dense block — [FrameOS 2026 guide](https://frameos.studio/blog/how-to-add-subtitles-in-capcut)
- Premiere Pro 26.3 supports word-by-word captions natively: transcription → upgrade captions to graphics → animation presets, plus per-word color, glow and flicker — [Kyler Holland blog](https://www.kylerholland.com/blog/single-word-captions-premiere-pro-tutorial)
- An Adobe community feature request says Premiere's auto captions are accurate but lacked animated styling compared with CapCut and DaVinci Resolve — [Adobe Community](https://community.adobe.com/feature-requests-730/animated-auto-captioning-for-premiere-pro-1328790)
- Paid Premiere plugins for animated captions and auto-clipping include Premiere Assistant (formerly Cutback), FireCut and Brevidy. These sources are vendor pages — [Cutback](https://cutback.video/blog/how-to-create-animated-captions-in-premiere-pro-(no-after-effects-needed)); [FireCut](https://firecut.ai/blog/how-to-add-viral-animated-captions-in-premiere-pro/)

### Inferences
- In 2025–2026, animated word-by-word captions are mostly taught in **single viral-style tutorial videos** and by **caption-tool vendors** (Submagic, FireCut, Cutback, OpusClip). Big "editing school" channels teach them less. For the user, CapCut is the easiest route: one free app with built-in word-by-word caption modes.
- Justin Odisho and Cinecom are solid for **Premiere Pro skills**: text effects, transitions, zooms and masking. They are general editing channels, not clipping channels, and I found no 2025–2026 Shorts-caption video from either.
- Because Premiere Pro only recently added native word-by-word animation (26.3), Premiere caption tutorials older than 2026 will mostly show workarounds or plugins. Newer videos are preferable.

## Q2. Which channels teach the strategy of clip channels (picking moments, hooks, titles, retention for Shorts)?

### Takeaway
The strongest verified Shorts-strategy teacher is **Jenny Hoyos** (~12.4M subs, Oct 2026): hooks in 3 seconds or less, power words, foreshadowing and 90%+ retention. Most of her teaching is in interviews on other channels and in paid programs, not tutorials on her own channel. Whop "Content Rewards" clipping tutorials are plentiful in 2025–2026, but their channels could not be identified and most are earnings-hype content. For finding moments, many videos lean on AI tools (OpusClip, Vizard, StreamLadder ClipGPT, NexusClips).

### Inferences
- For **hooks and retention**, Jenny Hoyos's rules (hook in 3 seconds or less, a promise and foreshadowing, then a payoff) transfer directly to podcast clips. Start the clip on the most shocking line, then let the story pay off.
- Whop clipping tutorials are mostly about **getting paid**, not editing quality. Their editing advice (CapCut, captions, SFX, music, head tracking) matches the standard clip-channel style, so the user can learn the format from them but should ignore the income claims.
- For streamer clips, the core skills are the **facecam-top or gameplay-bottom layout** and **finding chat-spike or reaction moments**. AI tools like StreamLadder and OpusClip can do the first draft, and manual edits add the meme, SFX and zoom style.

- No reliable data was found on what titles or hooks perform best specifically for podcast-clip or streamer-clip pages.
